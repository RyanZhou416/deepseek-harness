- Settled per-team lock queues release their map entries after the final waiter, preventing unbounded retention across short-lived teams.
- Captain and member prompt/tool capabilities remain stable across planning, approval, execution, compaction, cold restoration, and archive operations.
- Web approval wakes the Captain after the plan commits and scheduling begins.
- Existing teams are continued instead of recreated when a new activation request arrives.

- Resident members receive Host Steer at the nearest model step; inactive members receive Host Queue for a distinct cold-resumable turn.
- Unread Captain mailbox records survive offline delivery and are claimed, steered, and acknowledged after the Captain becomes resident.
- Retired members remain available for transcript history while Host delivery and public adjacent-Agent messaging reject new work.
- The bounded unread-mailbox projection cache, fallback persistence, parked-attempt recovery, Windows filesystem retries, and Web activity presentation remain intact.
- The private package, peer declarations, development dependencies, and transitive DSH overrides pin `0.1.5-alpha.2`.
